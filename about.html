// About Me

Who I Am

An engineer at the intersection of control theory, vehicle dynamics, and autonomous driving

Mohammadaadil Shaikh

I am a Master's student in Electromobility – ACES (Autonomous, Connected, Electrified, Shared) at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Germany. My academic and project work focuses on the development of algorithms and models for Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and control systems.

My current research at the Chair of Automatic Control (FAU) involves road friction estimation using both physics-based tire models and deep learning approaches on a Steer-by-Wire vehicle platform. I have hands-on experience with sensor fusion, state estimation (EKF/UKF), Model Predictive Control, and real-time control implementation on dSpace hardware.

With a foundation in Automobile Engineering and a passion for bridging classical control theory with modern machine learning, I aim to contribute to the development of safer and more intelligent vehicles.
